Frequently Asked Questions

Does this require a monthly fee?

BLE finding tags rely on compatible phones and a supported finding network rather than a cellular connection on the tag. Confirm the supplied model, app requirements and applicable service terms; no lifetime fee guarantee is made.

Is it compatible with Android?

Please confirm Android Find Hub support for the exact model and firmware before ordering. Apple Find My support, switching between networks and simultaneous pairing must be verified separately.

Can I print my own brand logo?

Yes, we specialize in OEM services. For orders above 500 units, we provide custom logo printing and retail packaging.
